Welcome to the Humblewood Table

Session 0

A quick guide to our tone, boundaries, characters, and table rules.

This is a collaborative story. Please tell the DM privately about anything you would like handled more gently; you never need to justify a boundary.

The tone of the campaign

The Wood can be warm and whimsical, but it is not always comfortable.

  • Missing people
  • Hallucinations or altered perception
  • Decay, death, and grief
  • Unsettling imagery
  • Characters behaving strangely or creepily (no sexual violence)

Our safety tools

You are always allowed to pause, step away, or ask for a change.

  • Message the DM if you need a moment or feel overwhelmed.
  • Use the anonymous safety button on the website to notify the DM privately.
  • When the button is used, we will take a 15-minute break.
  • Other safety requests and ideas are very welcome.

Boundaries check-in

Let the DM know privately if you have boundaries around any of these topics:

  • Violence toward children or children in danger
  • Body horror
  • Animal harm
  • Insects
  • Starvation or dehydration
  • Grief and dead family members
  • Mind control, hallucinations, or memory alteration
  • Romance and sexual content
  • PvP and intra-party conflict

A boundary can be a hard no, a request for a fade-to-black, or simply a heads-up before a scene. All of those are useful.

Make a character who gets involved

Your character should have a reason to be curious about the Wood. When something genuinely wrong happens, they should also have some reason to get involved.

Curiosity is a great beginning. Give your character one thread that pulls them toward the mystery.

If you miss a session

If only one person is absent, we will generally continue unless it is a major session. Please let the DM know if that is a problem for you.

Your character can be on a “side quest” or have an “ill day.” Depending on what you choose, they may even receive a small buff for the next session—for example, spending the day working out and sparring with the weapons master could grant advantage on one Strength or hit roll.

Questions, boundaries, or requests? Message the DM anytime. 💚
