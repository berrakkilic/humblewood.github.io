How to arrange the stones

  1. Drag each tablet from the left-hand pile into the wooden frame.
  2. Double-click, right-click, press R, or use the Rotate button to turn the selected tablet.
  3. When a correctly oriented tablet reaches its old position, it settles and glows.
  4. Place all eight tablets to complete a perfect square.

Keyboard: choose a tablet, use the arrow keys to move it, hold Shift for smaller steps, and press R to rotate.
