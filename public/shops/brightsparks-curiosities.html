Tools & contraptions

“Precision” timekeeper

2 gp

Runs for an hour. Usually. May be off by 5–10 minutes.

Bell-Wire Alarm

8 sp

Twenty feet of wire. Spend 1 minute rigging it; a creature crossing it rings the bell unless it spots the wire with a DC 12 Perception check. Reusable if the wire or contraption is not damaged.

Brightspark’s Third Hand

1 gp

An articulated rod with a little gripping claw and clamp. Holds a torch, mirror, potion, or tool while leaving your hands free. Cannot attack anything or manipulate complicated objects.

Pocket Listening Cone

8 sp

Press it against a door or thin wall to gain advantage on one Perception check to hear through it. Anything very loud on the other side may hurt your ears.

Fold-Out Looking Mirror

1 gp

Look around corners without exposing your head (grants advantage on that stealth check). While deliberately scouting this way, creatures around the corner cannot see you simply because you are observing them.

Spring-Snapper

6 sp

Wind this tiny noise-maker and choose an appropriate amount of time. It makes a sharp clack after that delay, creating an excellent distraction. One use until rewound.

Brightspark Emergency Grappler

2 gp

Fires a tiny hooked line 20 feet. It cannot bear any creature’s full weight, but can retrieve small objects, pull a lever, snag keys, or drag an empty bag.
